Privacy Policy

Overview

SizeDrop is a browser-based file compression tool. This static version is designed so that image processing occurs locally on your device. PDF processing is also performed in the browser after the PDF is selected.

Files

SizeDrop does not intentionally upload your selected files to a SizeDrop server in this static version. Temporary browser object URLs are used to process and download files and are released when they are no longer needed.

Analytics and advertising

If analytics or Google AdSense are added later, this policy should be updated before those services are enabled. Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and your applicable choices.

Third-party libraries

The application may load open-source libraries from public CDNs. Your browser may connect to those CDN providers to retrieve the library files.

Children

Do not use the service to process files containing information you are not authorized to process.
